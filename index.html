How to Use This App

  • Click on any letter in the grid to learn about it.
  • Use the "Prev" and "Next" buttons to navigate through the alphabet.
  • Click the "Speak" button to hear the letter and word pronounced.
  • Use the category buttons to switch between topics like Animals and Food.
  • Toggle the "Animal Sounds" switch to hear fun sounds (coming soon!).
